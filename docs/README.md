# Study documentation

The durable research documents are organized by the question they answer:

- [`research_plan.md`](research_plan.md): hypotheses, arms, evaluation design,
  and statistical plan.
- [`dataset_audit.md`](dataset_audit.md): dataset roles, licensing boundaries,
  split preservation, and leakage controls.
- [`literature_matrix.md`](literature_matrix.md): prior work and the study gap.
- [`research_completion_matrix.md`](research_completion_matrix.md): completed
  claims, evidence, and remaining limitations.
- [`artifact_release_audit.md`](artifact_release_audit.md): what may and may
  not be published.
